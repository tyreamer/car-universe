# Substantive review checklist

This checklist is separate from automated validation. No human has completed it for the v2 cohort. The initial review was conducted by Codex and is labeled AI-reviewed, human review pending.

For both cars in a comparison:

- Open the cited passages. Confirm the publisher, original context, test configuration, market and model years. A search snippet or a title alone is insufficient.
- Separate observation, manufacturer assertion, specialist inference and assessor judgment. Record conflicts and unavailable sources without laundering them into confirmed facts.
- Check engine, gearbox, cooling, electrical systems and structural corrosion using comparable breadth. Appropriate scheduled maintenance is the common assumption. Separate age, storage, abuse and existing-condition exposure.
- For each material mechanism, review severity, affected years, preventability and residual risk. Identify incidence as unknown unless a relevant population and denominator support it. Bulletin coverage is not a failure rate.
- Review the same six cost scopes: scheduled work, consumables, repairs, age renewal, major-failure exposure and acquisition catch-up. Obtain documented parts/labor inputs and applicable scope before quoting dollars. A suggested reserve is not observed average spending.
- Check steering, chassis, gearshift, powertrain, brakes and ordinary-speed enjoyment against applicable firsthand evaluations. Do not copy a different engine or transmission's judgment.
- Review design observations independently of aesthetic preference. Identify whose taste a number expresses. Do not invent audience votes or promote My taste into the shared catalog.
- Choose a broad anchor or null. Explain differences, ties and unknowns. Add an adjustment only when its specific evidence and comparison justify finer precision.
- Recheck both sides whenever an ordering changes. Update decision revision, actual assessor/date, reason and evidence fingerprint, then regenerate and review the before/after report. An unchanged small gap does not exempt a pair from review.
- Inspect the rendered chart and profile, including unknown scores, overlap selection and a mobile viewport. Confirm the numeric upkeep direction and the distinction between personal taste and editorial scores.

Release procedure: run `npm run check`, execute the browser checks described in the README, inspect their screenshots, and review the research gaps. A successful build does not establish automotive accuracy. This correction branch must not be merged or deployed without the owner's instruction.
