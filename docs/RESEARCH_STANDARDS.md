# Research standards

1. Establish market, model years, chassis, body, engine and gearbox. Record only the selected configuration. Explain material differences where adjacent entries share displacement or gearbox.
2. Match eligible U.S. EPA rows by year, model label and powertrain. Preserve the selected row IDs. Consult an applicable period or manufacturer source for trim and chassis, and document disagreements.
3. Record output, weight and equipment with their stated year and measurement basis. Period test weights are specific to a tested vehicle. Leave unresolved fields null.
4. Separate routine upkeep, major repair exposure, prevention and observed failure incidence. A bulletin shows a documented issue and an applicability range. A single repair bill describes one vehicle.
5. Use applicable firsthand tests for driving and cover all six driving aspects. Design references support shape observations, not a numerical beauty claim. Label that separate judgment Editorial aesthetics; personal My taste values stay local.
6. Apply a broad rubric anchor only when the inspected evidence and comparative scope support it. Record the anchor, each justified adjustment, final arithmetic, assessor/type, date, review status, uncertainty and reference decisions. Low confidence does not repair missing evidence. Leave unsupported fields null.
7. Review both comparison partners using the same five-system durability and six-category upkeep checklists. Incidence remains unknown unless a relevant source measures it. Scheduled maintenance, prevention, age-related renewal and acquisition catch-up must remain distinct.
8. Dollar scenarios require scoped, documented inputs. A recommended reserve is not an observed average. Do not fabricate failure rates, statistical intervals or community votes.
9. Run validation and calibration checks, inspect the actual chart and review the generated change and coverage reports. A changed supported ordering requires a new explicit decision review; numerical distance alone is not a validity test.

The initial catalog includes a mixture of archived identities and EPA-supported model/powertrain matches awaiting trim-level corroboration. A research state records the kind of evidence present. It does not signify independent human approval. The date on an assessment records the stated assessor's review. Legacy evidence retained during migration is not automatically reinspected evidence.

Use [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md), [SCHEMA_V2.md](SCHEMA_V2.md) and the separate [substantive review checklist](SUBSTANTIVE_REVIEW.md). Automated checks enforce declared scope and derivation consistency; they cannot establish source truth or automotive accuracy.
